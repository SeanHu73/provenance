# Design Brief

**Role:** Freelance UI/UX + visual designer — design only, no coding
**Engagement:** One-off project, remote

---

## The project

We've built a self-guided walking tour app, with a few extra features layered on top of
the tour itself. It works, and we've styled it ourselves so far, but it's reached the
point where it really needs a proper design eye to pull everything together into
something coherent and genuinely lovely to use.

We'll give you a proper walkthrough of the product when we talk.

## What we're hoping for

There are two pieces to this.

The first is a **style guide**. We do have one, but it's thin and has drifted from what's
actually on screen, so we'd like you to replace it — colour, typography, spacing,
components and their states. We'd rather leave the scope to you than guess at it
ourselves, so please take it as far as you think it needs to go.

The second is a set of **UI/UX recommendations**: an annotated critique and proposal
covering what you'd change and why. It doesn't need to cover every screen. We'd much
rather have deep thinking on the ones that matter than a shallow pass over all of them.
We can point you at the screens we're least happy with, though we're just as curious to
hear what you'd flag that we haven't noticed ourselves.

There's no coding involved — we'll handle the implementation. It does help enormously if
you write for a developer who'll follow your specs closely but can't read your mind, so
exact values rather than "a warm grey", the awkward component states alongside the happy
ones, and a little of your reasoning where you've made a judgement call. That last one
matters more than it sounds: we'll be living with these decisions long after the project
ends, and we'd like to be able to extend them without quietly breaking your logic.

And please do be opinionated. Strong views we can push back on are far more useful to us
than safe suggestions we can't really act on.

## A few things worth knowing up front

It's **mobile-first**, and we really mean it — almost everyone uses this on a phone, so
390px is the width that matters.

It's also used **outdoors and on the move**: bright sunlight, one hand, often with
headphones in. That's probably the constraint our current design serves worst, and we'd
expect it to push you fairly hard on contrast and tap targets.

We treat WCAG AA contrast as a floor rather than a goal.

The app is built in **React with Tailwind**, and every themeable value already lives in a
CSS custom property — which means a **token-based** guide maps almost directly onto our
code, and saves us both a lot of translation.

## Format

Whatever a developer can build from. Figma would be ideal, but a deck, a PDF, or markdown
with images are all perfectly good — we'll leave that to you.

You won't need to produce any code, a coded design system, production assets, or an icon
library.

## What we'll give you

A build to click through, screenshots, our existing style guide, the current tokens and
fonts, and quick, straight answers to anything you want to ask.
